Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Minimalist Task Reminder ("the App") is developed by Sunny Kasam ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Summary: The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All your task data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

1. Information Collection

The App does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number) are collected
  • No location data is collected
  • No usage analytics or crash reports are collected
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No advertising identifiers are collected

2. Data Storage

All task data (task names, schedules, completion records) is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (Room/SQLite). This data is:

  • Stored only on your device's local storage
  • Never transmitted to any server or third party
  • Deleted when you uninstall the App

3. Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • Schedule Exact Alarm / Use Exact Alarm: Required to trigger task reminders at the exact time you set. No data is transmitted.
  • Post Notifications: Required to show reminder notifications on your device. Notifications are generated locally.
  • Vibrate: Required to vibrate your device when a reminder triggers.
  • Receive Boot Completed: Required to reschedule your alarms after your device restarts. No data is transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or cloud services. The App functions entirely offline.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties, because no user data is collected in the first place.

7. Data Security

Your task data is stored in your device's private application storage, which is sandboxed by the Android operating system. Other apps cannot access this data under normal circumstances.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
